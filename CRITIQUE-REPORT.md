### RULING 1 — λ = **0.0080 / hour**
Three values exist, all per-hour: `0.0077` (90.0h half-life, 15 sites) · `0.00800` (86.6h, 4) · `0.01`
(69.3h, 14). **The tie-break is not "what the contract says" — CONTRACT.md contradicts itself.** It
declares `lambda: 0.0077` (:237/:302/:441) but publishes `recency: 0.9261` at `age_hours: 9.6` (:52), and
`−ln(0.9261)/9.6 = 0.0079972`. That recency is load-bearing on `score: 1.5002` (:178/:199) and
`top_margin: 0.9782` (:240). λ=0.0077 → 1.5045; λ=0.01 → 1.4717. **Both break the contract's own hero
arithmetic.** The published *output* pins λ — which invalidates ARCHITECTURE.md:963's stated reason for
shipping 0.0077.

> **Build-critical side effect:** re-pinning λ **flips the sign** of PLAN.md set-piece 3's margin
> (`+0.0023` → `−0.0074`). The verdict stays UNDECIDED, so no guarantee changes — but if the demo
> narrates "the incoming claim wins by a hair", **re-seed the trusts; never re-tune λ.**

**ε has NO conflict.** 0.05 is uniform corpus-wide; the other `0.05` (ARCHITECTURE.md:725, :2085-2087) is
the trust clamp floor — a name collision. **The ledger row overstated this half.**

### RULING 2 — collapse `FLOW` + `DERIVED_FROM` into a single `:FLOW`, kind in a property
AF-4 caps `algo.maxFlow` at one relationship type. Option (b) *two calls and combine* is **unsound, not
merely imprecise** — max-flow does not decompose over an edge-type partition, and a provenance chain
alternates types, so every single-type subgraph is disconnected at each transition and no combinator
recovers the union's max-flow. Option (c) projection is **unimplementable** — v4.22.1 has no
graph-catalog/projection procedure and Cypher cannot synthesize a relationship type. Node-split survives
intact (verified live on `menger2`: node-split → maxFlow 1 where the un-split echo → 2).

> **Consequence build must handle:** with `DERIVED_FROM` gone, `algo.SPpaths` runs `relTypes:['FLOW']`
> and therefore traverses split edges too. **Every `:FLOW` edge must carry BOTH `nl` and `cap`** (split
> edges get `nl: 0.0`). **New engine fact, measured live:** SPpaths with the *correct* `weightProp` over
> *property-less* edges **silently returns hop count** — a second, data-driven instance of the AF-3 trap,
> with no error. The `T-3b` guard (`pathWeight != pathCost`) catches it.
