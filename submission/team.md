## AI assistance

Claude Code assisted throughout: schema drafting, Cypher authoring, FastAPI route
implementation, test scaffolding, and documentation. The disclosure lives in `README.md`
as the rules require.

What assistance did not cover, and what therefore stands as the author's own work: the
graph model and the choice of Menger's theorem as the independence measure; the decision
to node-split every source, which is the load-bearing modelling move; the boot-time
controls that refuse to start the engine unless both the `1` and the `2` reproduce; and
the decision to publish `CLAIMS.md`, `BUILD-REPORT.md`, and
`submission/HONESTY-LEDGER.md` rather than a clean highlight reel. Every claim in this
bundle was re-derived by running a command, not by asking a model whether it was true.
